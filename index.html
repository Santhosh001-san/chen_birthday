My Dearest Friend

I used to talk to you so casually at first, but I don't know what kind of magic you worked on me. Without even realizing it myself, I have fallen in love with you.

In the beginning, you spoke with me so simply, but the kindness and affection you showed me afterward is something no one else has ever shown me. Nobody has ever called me handsome before, but you were the very first girl to call me handsome, right after my mother.

Then, when you didn't come online for about 20 days, I felt extremely sad wondering why you weren't coming back. It was during that time of longing that I realized my love for you. I realized it only then.

If you say no to me, it is not your fault at all. It is just my bad luck that seems to chase me constantly. I know that if I confess my love to you, you might not accept it, but still, I gathered all my courage from deep within and spoke my heart to you.

It is completely okay. You only shared what you truly felt, and that is more than enough for me. But I will tell you one thing: you and only you will remain in my heart. No one else will ever occupy your place. I would rather die single in the end than forget you.

Please do not think of this as me threatening you or just proposing randomly. These are words coming straight from the depth of my soul. Please take good care of your family and your health.

If you ever want to know more about me, I have already sent you all my details in the message where you mentioned it was too difficult to read. Everything is right there.

After you see this message, the choice is entirely yours. Even though I feel you might not love me back, I still must tell you: if you ever decide to love me, you must come to India, to Tamil Nadu. Yes, please come at least once and travel around our Tamil Nadu; you will get to know our land. By the time you see this message, I might have deleted this app. If I have said anything that hurt your heart, please forgive me.

Take care of your health, eat your meals on time, and eat healthy foods. If I ever come to China, will you show me around your home and your country? I am looking forward to coming there just for that. I will definitely find you.
